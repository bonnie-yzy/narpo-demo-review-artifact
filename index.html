01 / The paper

Abstract

Masked-token diffusion models for zero-shot TTS iteratively unmask acoustic tokens in parallel, yielding a dynamically changing state-action space that is not readily accommodated by existing autoregressive RL methods built around token-by-token trajectories. To address this challenge, we introduce NARPO (non-autoregressive relative policy optimization), an on-policy post-training method combining real-trace replay of parallel trajectories, lexicographic reward ranking, and late counterfactual branching for finer-grained credit assignment. We evaluate NARPO by post-training OmniVoice, a representative masked-token diffusion TTS foundation model. Controlled ablations show that the complete method achieves the lowest mean content error among RL variants while maintaining competitive speaker similarity and perceptual quality. Across eight guidance-free decoding budgets from four to 32 steps, NARPO retains 1.17–1.38% content error. At eight steps, it reduces content error from 2.42% to 1.22% and increases UTMOS from 3.11 to 3.62 relative to compute-matched CFG distillation.
